Release 4.2: what it did to the heroes

On 12 August 2026 the company changed how job offers reach its responders (we call them heroes here). Within days some heroes got fewer jobs, some missed offers they used to catch, and handlers filed a flood of tickets. This file follows the evidence.

1 Numbers2 Mission3 Asked vs built4 Villains5 Health lab6 Orders7 Loose ends

How to read this file

An offer (the company calls it a “ping”) is one job request sent to one hero’s phone. The hero can take it, turn it down, or not answer in time, which we call a miss. Each hero has a hidden score for “said yes lately”, and the software uses it to decide who gets asked first.

OBSERVED counted straight from the offer history, ticket list, interviews or files ESTIMATED worked out or inferred by us

“Before” means 29 June to 11 August (44 days). “After” means 12 August to 6 September (26 days) for offers, and to 7 September (27 days) for tickets. Only data from the Rook wiki, the Rook database and the files in this folder is used. Nobody here is identified beyond their public cover name.
