# Interview guide: the two handlers nobody interviewed

**Who:** Linda Pruitt (handler for Farlight) and Desmond Okafor (handler for The Undertow).
**Why them:** together they filed 40 of the 147 tickets, and 20 of them are about their hero going quiet. Neither was among the four handlers interviewed in September. (Source: support tickets table, interview list in the wiki.)
**Length:** about 30 minutes. Use cover names only. Do not ask who anyone really is, or about anyone's household (Security Policy 4.1).

## Before the call
- Re-read their own tickets, so you can use their words back to them. Linda: "Nothing has come through for Farlight since Friday 28 August." Desmond: "He asked me this morning whether his account is still active."
- Have one thing ready to show if they ask: their hero's offers by week, from the pings table. Don't open with it.
- Ask permission to take notes.

## Seven questions
1. **The last check.** Tell me about the last time you looked into [Farlight / The Undertow]. What did you open, and what did you see?
2. **A normal week.** Before the middle of August, what did a normal week look like for them? Roughly how many jobs, at what times of day?
3. **First noticing.** When did you first notice something was different, and what made you notice it?
4. **The last offer.** Tell me about the last time a job did reach them. What happened, step by step, from the phone going off to the end?
5. **What they say.** What does your hero say or ask when it's quiet, and what do you tell them?
6. **What you tried.** What have you tried so far (settings, hours, tags, contacting us)? What happened each time?
7. **One change.** If one thing on your screen could change to help with this, what would it be? And is there anything we haven't asked that you expected us to?

## Keep it open
- **Don't suggest a cause.** Don't mention the 60-second wait, scores or the release. Let them name what they think happened.
- **Don't promise a fix.** Say what you'll do with their answers and when you'll get back to them.
- **Use their words.** Follow up with "Say more about that" or "What happened next?"
- **Park Supply questions.** Gear, requisitions and catalog search go on a separate list.

## After the call
- Write down: what they said unprompted, what they said only when asked, and one quote each.
- Check: did either describe the offer vanishing before they could answer (Q4)? That would back the 60-second wait. Did either describe nothing arriving at all? That would back the score knock-on.
- Reply to their open tickets, whatever the answer. Both are still waiting.
