# The one number for Helen

## The number
**4 of 16 heroes have not taken a single offer in 18 to 23 days. The other 12 took one within the last day.**
*(As of 6 September, the last day in the data. Source: pings table.)*

## Why this and not the weekly acceptance rate
The weekly acceptance rate recovered to 75.2% in the first days of September, close to the old 76.6%. That makes it look like the problem is fading. It isn't: the four quiet heroes now barely appear in that rate, because they are barely being asked. The headline average hides the people who were hurt most.

## What the number can't tell her
- **Why.** It shows who is shut out, not the cause. The cause (the 60-second wait, then a score that never recovers) is an estimate, because the database has no scores or answer times.
- **Whether they were asked.** The Undertow and Meteor Mite were offered a job as recently as 5 September and missed it. Farlight was last offered one on 28 August, Vesper on 1 September. So "not taken" is a mix of "rarely asked" and "asked, but too late to answer."
- **What happens next.** The data ends on 6 September. Anything since then is not in this number.
- **Sample size.** Sixteen heroes is a small group.

## The sentence to say out loud
"Twelve of our sixteen heroes took a job yesterday. Four haven't taken one for about three weeks, and the average we report doesn't show them."
